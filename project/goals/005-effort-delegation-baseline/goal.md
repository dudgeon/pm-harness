# 005 · Effort and delegation baseline

## Why
The value case rests on two hypotheses: H-003 (most PM effort goes to work that doesn't need
judgment) and H-004 (missing context drives that effort). We also need a way to measure progress,
both the shift in effort (H-006) and the level of delegation (H-005). This goal answers Q-005 and
Q-010.

## Definition of done
_Vague:_ An evidence-based picture of where PM effort goes, broken down by move (goal 003), that
supports or undercuts H-003 and H-004. Plus a measurement framework for effort shift, delegation
level, and context credibility that a pilot could use.

## Requirements
- R1: Use the taxonomy (goal 003) as the unit of analysis.
- R2: Draw on Geoff's own experience and observations, interviews or surveys with PMs, and
  published studies. Calendar and time-tracking data aren't available (Geoff, 2026-09-27).
- R3: Enterprise findings enter the repo only as generic summaries (C5).
- R4: Users have to be willing to accept the measurement framework (C6, Q-013). If trace capture
  feels like surveillance, it fails the earned-adoption test.

## Constraints
- Inherits C1–C8.

## Assumptions
- H-003 and H-004 (both under test here).
- Interviews and surveys can estimate effort shares well enough to be useful: a hypothesis. Self-
  reports may be imprecise, so treat the results as directional.

## Non-goals
- A formal time-and-motion study.
- Evaluating individual PMs' performance.

## Open
- Sample size, and who to interview. C5 limits what we can record.
- Which delegation scale to adopt: the draft in [path](../../context/wiki/path.md#delegation-levels),
  Feng et al.'s levels, or Anthropic's trace-based measures.
