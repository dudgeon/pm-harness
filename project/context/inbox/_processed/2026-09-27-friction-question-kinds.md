# `open-questions.md` mixes two kinds of question

The Q format (`between: A ↔ B`) fits contradictions well. It fits big research questions awkwardly.
"What are the PM moves?" isn't a conflict between two sources. It's the charter of a workstream.
Q-004 to Q-010 are this kind of question, and Q-011 to Q-014 are genuine tensions.

**Options:**
- Add a `kind: key question | tension` field.
- Split the file into two sections.
- Let goals own their research questions, and keep `open-questions.md` for tensions only.

**Harness implication (H-001):** a PM agent probably needs the same distinction. Questions to
research become work. Contradictions to resolve become decisions.
